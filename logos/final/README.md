# Golden Blocks Mission — Final Logos

There are two standalone logos. Each one is a complete design of its own.

| Logo | Showcase | Shirt mockups |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold Bars** | `showcase_gold-bars.png` | `shirts_gold-bars.png` |
| **Emblem Seal** | `showcase_emblem-seal.png` | `shirts_emblem-seal.png` |

## Transparent background (Gold Bars)
- `golden-blocks_gold-bars-blocks-only_*`: just the four gold blocks, no background, no text (`full`, `flat`, `mono-dark`, `mono-white`)
- `golden-blocks_gold-bars-transparent_*`: blocks + name, no background (`full` / `flat` with dark text for light backgrounds, `full-on-dark` with light text, plus `mono-dark` / `mono-white`)

## Versions (in `svg/` and `png/`)
- `_full`: shaded gold, glow and shine. For screens, social media and digital/DTG printing.
- `_flat`: solid colours only. **Use this one for screen-printed shirts, embroidery and vinyl.**
- `_mono-dark`: one dark ink.
- `_mono-white`: one white ink, for coloured shirts.

Send printers the **SVG** files. They are vectors and the text is already converted to shapes, so no fonts are needed.
The PNG files are 3000 px with a transparent background.

Colours: mustard `#D9A21B` (shades `#EDBE45` / `#B5850F`), dark grey `#3A3D42`, light grey `#E6E7E9`.
To regenerate the files, see the comments at the top of `src/final.mjs` and `src/render.mjs`.
