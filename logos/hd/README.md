# Golden Blocks Mission — HD collection (3D blocks)

These four concepts follow the style of the reference image: 3D yellow blocks with a dark cross channel, on a light grey field, with the name on one line and NORTH EAST KENYA FIELD below.

1. **Orbit**: a gold ring sweeps around the blocks. It passes behind at the top and in front at the bottom.
2. **Isometric**: four rectangular blocks in 3D on a dark base. The gaps between them form a cross.
3. **Sunrise**: the blocks rise from the horizon with gold rays behind them.
4. **Field Pin**: a gold location pin holding the blocks, meaning the mission is planted in North East Kenya.

5. **Blocks**: the Orbit design with the ring removed, showing just the blocks and the name.
6. **Blocks, no text**: only the four blocks, tightly cropped, with no background (`_transparent`, `_flat`).

Versions: `_field` (light grey background), `_transparent` (no background), `_flat` (solid colours for screen printing and embroidery).
**Square (1:1):** every design also exists as `golden-blocks_square_*`, with the mark enlarged above the name, as 4000 × 4000 px PNGs and SVGs. These are also uploaded to Canva (Uploads).

Landscape PNGs are 4000 × 2500 px in `png/`. Send printers the SVGs in `svg/`, which are vectors and can scale to any size.

Colours: yellow #FFC93C, field #BFC1C4, grey #7C7F86 (lines). The text uses a darker charcoal (#34373C / #4B4E55) so it is readable on the grey field.
To regenerate: see the comments at the top of `src/hd.mjs` and `src/render.mjs`.
